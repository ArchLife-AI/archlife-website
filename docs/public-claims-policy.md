# ArchLife Public Claims Policy

## Purpose

ArchLife public surfaces should make useful work legible without overstating its
maturity, evidence, or operational status. This policy applies to websites,
product demos, decks, articles, and outreach material.

## The Rule

Every public claim about a metric, partner, outcome, capability, or clinical
context must be one of the following:

1. **Verified:** supported by a current source or a documented first-party
   record that the team can produce on request.
2. **Illustrative:** explicitly labelled as synthetic, fictional, example, or
   planning assumption at the point where it is shown.
3. **Removed:** when it is neither verified nor safely illustrative.

## Required Boundaries

- A public demo must state when its figures, records, scenarios, and market
  examples are illustrative or synthetic.
- Do not name an organisation as a customer, partner, target, pilot, or
  comparator without a verified basis and permission where appropriate.
- Do not present a prototype as a production system, live database, or client
  record.
- Do not imply clinical advice, diagnosis, monitoring, therapy, crisis support,
  or patient-data handling unless the product is designed, governed, and
  authorised for that use.
- Do not promise employment, salary, placement, hiring preference, commercial
  return, or operational outcome without evidence and explicit qualification.

## Review Before Release

For each changed public page, the owner checks:

1. Can this claim be evidenced today?
2. If it is an example, does the surrounding page say so plainly?
3. Could a reasonable reader infer an untrue relationship, guarantee, or
   clinical capability?
4. Does the page link back to the appropriate ArchLife context and contact
   route?

If any answer is uncertain, soften the wording or remove the claim before
publishing.

## Ongoing Review

Review public claims when a product changes materially and at least quarterly.
Remove stale numbers, names, and statements before adding new promotional copy.
